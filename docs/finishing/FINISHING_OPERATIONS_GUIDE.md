# SHUBHAM FABRICS MES — FINISHING OPERATIONS GUIDE

## 1. Quick Start for Finishing Supervisors & Operators

### Logging In
1. Navigate to `/login`.
2. Click the **Finishing** station card.
3. Enter PIN `1234` and click **Enter Station**.

### Receiving Inward Garments
1. Go to **Incoming (Stitching)** from the sidebar (`/finishing/incoming`).
2. Click **Confirm Receipt** on the incoming Challan.
3. Enter the physical bundle count, check for discrepancies, and confirm.

### Processing Garments on the Floor
1. Go to **Finishing Floor** (`/finishing/my-work`).
2. Select a received job, click **Allocate Station & Start**, choose finishing table / iron station.
3. Once operations are complete, click **Record Production Output**.
4. Enter Good Finished, Re-trim/Clean, and Scrap counts. Ensure sum equals input.
5. Perform **Finishing Quality Inspection** to verify thread trimming cleanliness, pressing, and dimensional fit.

### Transferring Output Downstream
1. Go to **Issue Challan** (`/finishing/issue-challan`).
2. Select the approved job and choose the destination department (defaults to `BUTTON_ATTACHMENT` or `PACKING`).
3. Click **Issue Handover Challan** and print the Code-128 barcode sticker card.
