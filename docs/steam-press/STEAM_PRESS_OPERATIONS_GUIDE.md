# STEAM PRESS OPERATOR'S MANUAL & WORKFLOW GUIDE
## Shubham Fabrics MES

### 1. Inwarding Garments from Finishing
1. Log into the MES using the **Steam Press** station button or username `steampress` and PIN `1234`.
2. Go to **Incoming (Finishing)** from the sidebar menu.
3. Click **Inward Bundle** on the arriving bundle.
4. Count physical pieces. Enter counted quantity.
5. If count matches, click **Confirm Inward**. Bundle moves to your active floor queue.

### 2. Pressing on the Factory Floor
1. Go to **Steam Press Floor** (`/steam-press/my-work`).
2. Select your job from the queue. Click **Start Pressing & Assign Table**.
3. Choose Table (e.g. Table 1, Vacuum Table, Buck Press).
4. Once pressing is finished, click **Record Pressing Output** or open **Focus Mode** (`/steam-press/workspace/[id]`).
5. Confirm Good pieces, Re-press pieces (if any), and Scorch/Reject pieces (if any).
6. Click **Confirm Output**.

### 3. Handover to Final Quality Check
1. Click **Issue Outgoing Challan** (`/steam-press/issue-challan`).
2. Verify target department is **Final Quality Check** (`FINAL_QC`).
3. Verify quantity to dispatch.
4. Click **Issue Handover Challan**.
5. Print the Code-128 barcode sticker card and affix to the bundle crate before handing over to Final QC.
