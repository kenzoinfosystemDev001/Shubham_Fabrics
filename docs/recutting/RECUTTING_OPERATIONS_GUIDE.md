# Recutting Operations Guide

**Document Version:** 1.0.0  
**Department:** Panel & Embroidery Recutting Section (`RECUTTING`)  

---

## 1. Quick Start for Floor Operators

1. **Sign In**: Navigate to `/login`, click the **Recutting** station tile (or type username `recutting`, PIN `1234`).
2. **Review Incoming Work**:
   - Go to **Incoming Challans** (`/recutting/incoming`).
   - Locate the Challan issued by Washing QC (`WASHING_QC`).
   - Verify piece count against physical lot on the trolley.
   - Click **Receive Lot**.
3. **Open Cutting Workspace**:
   - Go to **My Work** (`/recutting/my-work`).
   - Find the received job in the queue.
   - Click **Start Recutting**.
   - Review the Wilcom design number, embroidery placement, and panel boundaries.
   - Click **View Tech Sheet** to inspect the interactive spec sheet if needed.
4. **Perform Precision Cuts**:
   - Use the precision cutter / shears along the marked perimeter.
   - Ensure seam allowances match specifications for Stitching assembly.
5. **Record Output**:
   - Enter good cut panels, re-trims, and any damaged cuts.
   - Confirm balance is 0.
   - Click **Complete Operation**.
6. **Handover to Stitching**:
   - Click **Handover to Stitching**.
   - Review the generated Challan (`CH-RC-XXXXXX`).
   - Print or view barcode stickers and dispatch the lot to the Stitching line.
